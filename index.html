YEEUN HONG

I am a Sociology Ph.D. candidate at the University at Albany, specializing in immigration, race and ethnicity, gender and family, and Asian and Asian American Studies.

My research examines how immigration policies constrain or enable family formation and intimate relationship-building processes for Asian migrants in the United States.
